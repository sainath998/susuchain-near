The savings circle,
without the treasurer.

Over a billion people save in rotating circles: everyone pays in each round and one member takes the pot. SusuChain keeps the pot in a NEAR contract that pays out automatically, covers missed payments from collateral, and turns every on-time payment into a portable savings record.

  1. Lock one contribution as collateral when you join.
  2. Payout order is shuffled on-chain when the circle fills. Nobody picks who goes first.
  3. Each round, the last payment in triggers the payout to that round's member.
  4. Missed the deadline? Anyone can settle; the missing share comes out of that member's collateral and is recorded.
  5. Finish the circle: unused collateral comes back and your record unlocks bigger circles.
